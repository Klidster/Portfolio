About Us

Café Lehte is a cosy late-night coffee shop created for students, creatives, and anyone who loves a calm, welcoming atmosphere. We want every visit to feel easy, comfortable, and warm, whether you are coming in for a quiet morning coffee, an afternoon catch-up with friends, or a late-night study session. From carefully made drinks to fresh pastries, our space is designed to help people slow down, recharge, and enjoy the simple comfort of a really good café experience.

The idea for Café Lehte began with a simple goal: to create a coffee shop that felt peaceful, inviting, and easy to enjoy. Inspired by the meaning of “Lehte,” which means “easy” in Albanian, the café was imagined as a place where great coffee, soft conversation, and a cosy setting come together naturally. What started as a small idea for a student-friendly and community-focused café grew into a space where people can study, relax, meet, and feel at home at any time of day.
